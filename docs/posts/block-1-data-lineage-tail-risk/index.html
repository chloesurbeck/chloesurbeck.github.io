Executive Stakes: Why Gaussian Assumptions Fail in Credit Tail Risk

Under Basel III/IV internal ratings-based (IRB) capital standards and Federal Reserve SR 11-7 model risk guidelines, capital adequacy hinges on accurately estimating unexpected losses at the 99.9th percentile (\(\text{VaR}_{99.9\%}\)). When upstream ETL pipelines silently coerce extreme loss observations or when First-Line models rely on thin-tailed Gaussian approximations, regulatory capital is systematically understated during macroeconomic stress regimes.

This validation dossier integrates knowledge from UBC MDS Block 1 (DSCI 511, 521, 523, 551) into a unified 3-act quantitative risk and validation study.

Act 1: The Quant Build (First Line)

1.1 Mathematical Formulation & Parametric Loss Calibration

We model portfolio loss severity \(L\) across heavy-tailed parametric families—specifically the Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) and Weibull distributions—contrasted against a baseline Gaussian fit:

\[ f_{\text{Pareto}}(x; \alpha, x_m) = \frac{\alpha \, x_m^\alpha}{x^{\alpha+1}}, \quad x \ge x_m \]

\[ \text{VaR}_{\alpha}(L) = \inf \{ l \in \mathbb{R} : P(L > l) \le 1 - \alpha \}, \quad \alpha = 0.999 \]

1.2 Baseline Tail Loss Estimation

  • Empirical vs. Parametric Fit: Calibration of maximum likelihood estimators (MLE) across historical loan charge-off severities.
  • Capital Requirement Delta: Quantifying the capital shortfall between normal/log-normal assumptions and heavy-tailed Pareto tail estimates at \(\alpha = 0.999\).

Act 2: The MRM Audit (Second Line)

2.1 Programmatic Data Lineage & Boundary Invariants (pytest)

Independent validation begins prior to parameter estimation by auditing data ingestion integrity: - Schema & Domain Assertions: Automated pytest suite verifying strict non-negativity of Exposure at Default (\(\text{EAD} \ge 0\)), bounded Loss Given Default (\(\text{LGD} \in [0, 1]\)), and zero silent NaN coercion during multi-table joins. - Outlier Censorship Audit: Detecting whether winsorization or truncation thresholds in first-line preprocessing artificially suppress tail variance.

2.2 Distributional Failure Stress Test

  • Benchmarking model breakdown under synthetic tail contamination and extreme macro loss clustering.

Act 3: The Governance Synthesis

3.1 Trade-off Decisions & Operational Limits

  • Model Selection Rationale: Why a transparent two-parameter heavy-tailed specification with hard ingestion assertions is favored over an unconstrained multi-parameter density estimator prone to tail overfitting.

3.2 Concrete Monitoring Mandates & MRAs

  • Population Stability Index (PSI): Trigger mandatory model recalibration if \(\text{PSI} > 0.20\) across quarterly origination cohorts.
  • Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) Tail Test: Flag a Matter Requiring Attention (MRA) if upper-decile empirical loss exceedance breaches the 99% confidence envelope across two consecutive quarters.
